# Qi Men Dun Jia Feng Shui

A specialized environmental auditing and forecasting framework based on Phann Sophearith's *Qi Men Dun Jia: Feng Shui* (Chinese Metaphysics Reference Series, Book 30). This system integrates astronomical timing, compass spatial sectors, and human activity to diagnose residential and commercial properties.

## Summary

This skill guides the end-to-end execution of Qi Men Feng Shui. It covers the two fundamental paradigms: dynamic remote forecasting using Hour Charts and static physical site audits using 1,080 pre-calculated Yang Mansion House Charts. It provides step-by-step chart selection protocols, palace component taxonomy, special formation analysis (Escapes, Artifices, Clashes), and diagnostic lookup rules across the 13 dedicated household sectors.

## When to Use

Use this skill when the user:

- Asks to audit, evaluate, or diagnose a home or office using Qi Men Feng Shui.
- Inquires about the two paradigms: Qi Men Feng Shui Forecasting vs. Qi Men Feng Shui Audit.
- Requests the correct Qi Men House Chart based on Move-in Period, House Facing, and Door Location.
- Asks how to superimpose the 9 Palaces onto an architectural blueprint.
- Wants to evaluate specific functional rooms (Master Bedroom, Home Office, Kitchen, Toilet, etc.).
- Asks about Qi Men formations in a property (e.g. Tian Dun, Di Dun, Da Ge, Xing Ge, Tombs).
- Questions classical discrepancies (e.g. apartment balcony facing vs. building facing, door location vs. door facing).

## 1. The Two Operational Paradigms

1. **Qi Men Feng Shui Forecasting (奇門風水預測學)** (p. a5):
   - A remote, offsite diagnostic tool used when the practitioner cannot visit the physical site.
   - Plotted using the **Hour Chart (時家奇門)** for the exact moment the client raises the inquiry.
   - **Default Calculation Method**: Cast all dynamic hour charts using the **Zhi Run Fa (置閏法 - Intercalation Method)** by default to determine Dun (Yang/Yin) and Ju (1–9), unless the user explicitly requests an alternative method (e.g., Chai Bu Fa or Mao Shan Fa).
   - Evaluates current energetic blockages, structural defects, and immediate health/wealth pressures.

2. **Qi Men Feng Shui Audit (奇門風水勘察)** (p. a6):
   - An onsite, structural diagnostic tool assessing the permanent environmental matrix of a building.
   - Uses pre-calculated **Yang Mansion House Charts (奇門陽宅定局)** across Periods 1 to 9 (pp. 1-450).
   - Determined strictly by three physical inputs: Move-in Period, House Facing Direction, and Main Door Location.

### 1.1 Hour Chart Calculation Engine: Zhi Run Fa (置閏法)

When plotting dynamic Hour Charts (時家奇門) for remote forecasting:
- **Default System**: Always apply **Zhi Run Fa (置閏法 - Intercalation Method)** as the standard default method.
- **Yuan Cycle Allocation**:
  - **Upper Yuan (Shang Yuan 上元)**: Jia/Ji days with Earthly Branches Zi, Wu, Mao, You (子午卯酉).
  - **Middle Yuan (Zhong Yuan 中元)**: Jia/Ji days with Earthly Branches Yin, Shen, Si, Hai (寅申巳亥).
  - **Lower Yuan (Xia Yuan 下元)**: Jia/Ji days with Earthly Branches Chen, Xu, Chou, Wei (辰戌丑未).
  - Each Yuan runs for 5 days (60 double-hours / 1 Fu 符).
- **Intercalation (置閏) Protocol**:
  - Track the divergence between the 60 Jia Zi day cycle and the 24 Solar Terms (Jie Qi 節氣).
  - When **Chao Shen (超神)** (Leader Stem leading the Solar Term) exceeds 9 days (reaching 10–14 days) prior to the Solstices, trigger **Zhi Run**: repeat the 15-day 3-Yuan cycle of the closing solar term (Mang Zhong 芒種 before Summer Solstice, or Da Xue 大雪 before Winter Solstice) to synchronize the calendar before switching Dun polarity.
- **Dun Polarity**:
  - **Yang Dun (陽遁)**: From Winter Solstice (Dong Zhi) to Summer Solstice (Xia Zhi) — charts proceed in ascending order (Ju 1 to 9).
  - **Yin Dun (陰遁)**: From Summer Solstice (Xia Zhi) to Winter Solstice (Dong Zhi) — charts proceed in descending order (Ju 9 to 1).

## 2. Chart Selection Algorithm (Onsite Audit)

Follow this 4-step sequence to identify the correct House Chart:

### Step 1: Establish the House Period (p. a8, a36)

- Follows the San Yuan (Three Cycles, Nine Periods) 20-year cycles:
  - Upper Cycle: Period 1 (1864-1883), Period 2 (1884-1903), Period 3 (1904-1923).
  - Middle Cycle: Period 4 (1924-1943), Period 5 (1944-1963), Period 6 (1964-1983).
  - Lower Cycle: Period 7 (1984-2003), Period 8 (2004-2023), Period 9 (2024-2043).
- **Rule**: Determined strictly by the year the current occupants **moved in**, not by construction date (p. a8).

### Step 2: Establish the House Facing Direction (p. a9-a16)

- **Landed Houses**: The primary facade or architectural orientation receiving main Yang Qi and natural light (p. a9).
- **High-Rise Units (Condos / Apartments)**: The facing direction of the unit's **main Balcony** defines the house facing (p. a10).
- **8 Broad Directions Rule**: Facing is categorized into the 8 broad 45-degree Trigrams (p. a12, a16):
  - South (Li 離): 157.6° - 202.5°
  - Southwest (Kun 坤): 202.6° - 247.5°
  - West (Dui 兌): 247.6° - 292.5°
  - Northwest (Qian 乾): 292.6° - 337.5°
  - North (Kan 坎): 337.6° - 22.5°
  - Northeast (Gen 艮): 22.6° - 67.5°
  - East (Zhen 震): 67.6° - 112.5°
  - Southeast (Xun 巽): 112.6° - 157.5°

### Step 3: Establish Main Door Location (p. a17-a19)

- Pinpoint the exact center pivot of the property's floor plan (p. a11).
- Superimpose the 24-Mountain template (15-degree increments).
- Identify the specific mountain where the physical door aperture is situated (p. a17).
- **Critical Pitfall**: Door Location is the spatial sector where the door sits relative to the center; it is NOT the compass direction the door faces when looking out (p. a19).

### Step 4: Index Retrieval & Blueprint Superimposition

- Locate the Period in the Master Index (pp. iv-xxi).
- Cross-reference the Facing Trigram with the Main Door 24-Mountain cluster.
- Master Clusters:
  1. [Zi N2, Ren N1, Chou NE1, Gui N3]
  2. [Yin NE3, Gen NE2, Hai NW3, Qian NW2]
  3. [Mao E2, Jia E1, Xu NW1, Xin W3]
  4. [Chen SE1, Yi E3, You W2, Geng W1]
  5. [Si SE3, Xun SE2, Shen SW3, Kun SW2]
  6. [Wu S2, Bing S1, Wei SW1, Ding S3]
- Turn to the designated page (pp. 1-450) and superimpose the 9 Palaces over the scaled architectural blueprint (p. a21).

## 3. Structural Anatomy of a Plotted Palace

Each palace block contains three cosmic layers and dynamic modifiers:

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  Heaven Stem (天盤)  │ 9 Stars (九星) │ 8 Deities (八神) │
│                      │               │                 │
│  Special Formations & Timeliness Modifiers   │
│  (e.g., Ri Wang, Xing Shi, Dun, Zha, Ge)     │
│                                              │
│  Earth Stem (地盤)   │ 8 Doors (八門) │ Palace Number   │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 3.1 The 9 Stars (Heaven Plate - Timing & Quality)

- **Tian Peng (天蓬 Water 1)**: Financial boldness, high liquidity, risk, intellect.
- **Tian Rui (天芮 Earth 2)**: Students, sickness, physical body, training, obstinacy.
- **Tian Chong (天沖 Wood 3)**: Impulsive movement, sports, breakthrough, aggression.
- **Tian Fu (天輔 Wood 4)**: Academic achievement, mentorship, cultural refinement.
- **Tian Qin (天禽 Earth 5)**: Supreme balance, imperial authority, central coordination.
- **Tian Xin (天心 Metal 6)**: Executive leadership, medical solutions, strategy.
- **Tian Zhu (天柱 Metal 7)**: Oratory, debate, litigation, destructive speech, legal matters.
- **Tian Ren (天任 Earth 8)**: Perseverance, honesty, asset accumulation, real estate.
- **Tian Ying (天英 Fire 9)**: Visibility, fame, beauty, temper, public relations.

### 3.2 The 8 Doors (Man Plate - Tangible Human Action)

- **Open Door (開門 Metal 6)**: Auspicious. Career openings, business ventures, leadership.
- **Rest Door (休門 Water 1)**: Auspicious. Recuperation, relationship harmony, noble help, peace.
- **Life Door (生門 Earth 8)**: Auspicious. Profit, property acquisition, vitality, wealth rebirth.
- **Harm Door (傷門 Wood 3)**: Inauspicious. Competition, physical injury, litigation, debt collection.
- **Delusion Door (杜門 Wood 4)**: Neutral/Inauspicious. Concealment, roadblocks, secrecy, technical study.
- **Scenery Door (景門 Fire 9)**: Neutral/Auspicious. Fame, contracts, plans, intellectual property.
- **Death Door (死門 Earth 2)**: Inauspicious. Stagnation, endings, stubbornness, grief.
- **Fear Door (驚門 Metal 7)**: Inauspicious. Anxiety, lawsuits, doubts, verbal disputes.

### 3.3 The 8 Deities (Spirit Plate - Archetypal Forces)

- **Chief (值符 Zhi Fu)**: Supreme protection, mentors, dissolves misfortune.
- **Surging Snake (騰蛇 Teng She)**: Deception, nightmares, mental distress, restlessness.
- **Great Moon (太陰 Tai Yin)**: Secret blessings, hidden help, female benefactors, discretion.
- **Six Harmony (六合 Liu He)**: Marriage, partnerships, collaborative agreements, networking.
- **Hook (勾陳 Gou Chen) / White Tiger (白虎 Bai Hu)**: Physical injury, litigation, roadblocks.
- **Red Phoenix (朱雀 Zhu Que)**: Written documents, marketing, arguments, communication.
- **Nine Earth (九地 Jiu Di)**: Grounding, real estate, stealth, quiet asset accumulation.
- **Nine Heaven (九天 Jiu Tian)**: Ambition, vision, dynamism, open expansion.

## 4. Interaction Formations & Modifiers

### 4.1 The Nine Escapes (九遁 Jiu Dun)

- **Tian Dun (天遁)**: Bing + Open Door + Auspicious Star. Brings leadership prestige and executive aid.
- **Di Dun (地遁)**: Yi + Auspicious Door + Jiu Di. Concealed foundation building, real estate gain.
- **Ren Dun (人遁)**: Ding + Rest Door + Tai Yin. Smooth negotiations, talent recruitment.
- **Feng Dun (風遁)**: Auspicious Door in Xun Palace. Rapid reputation spread, dynamic execution.
- **Yun Dun (雲遁)**: Yi + Auspicious Door + Heaven/Clouds. Protective holding pattern, strategic patience.
- **Long Dun (龍遁)**: Yi or Kan Water + Life Door. Liquid investments, water business, territory gain.
- **Hu Dun (虎遁)**: Geng + Metal + Life Door in Qian/Gen. Legal dominance, executive authority.
- **Shen Dun (神遁)**: Spiritual protection, intuitive insights.
- **Gui Dun (鬼遁)**: Covert actions, surprising competitors via indirect means.

### 4.2 The Three Artifices (三詐) & Four Borrowings (四假)

- **Zhen Zha (真詐)**: Auspicious Door + Tai Yin. Ideal for private negotiations.
- **Xiu Zha (休詐)**: Auspicious Door + Liu He. Ideal for joint ventures and dispute resolution.
- **Chong Zha (重詐)**: Auspicious Door + Jiu Di. Ideal for long-term asset accumulation.
- **Tian Jia (天假)**: Borrowing institutional or high-level power.
- **Di Jia (地假)**: Leveraging property or geographic advantages.
- **Shen Jia (神假)**: Utilizing psychological or perception leverage.
- **Gui Jia (鬼假)**: Operating through proxies and third-party channels.

### 4.3 Inauspicious Formations & Tombs

- **Xing Ge (刑格)**: Geng punishments. Causes self-sabotage, penalties, legal violations.
- **Da Ge (大格)**: Geng + Gui. Abrupt blockages, structural collisions, broken deals.
- **Fu Ge (伏格)**: Geng + Bing. Aggressive clashes, forced retreats, explosive tension.
- **Fei Gong (飛宮) / Fu Gong (伏宮)**: Palace displacements, resident instability.
- **Zhan Ge (戰格)**: Fierce competitive combat and domestic warfare.
- **Zhu Que Tou Jiang (朱雀投江)**: Ding + Gui. Lost contracts, litigation, slander.
- **Teng She Jiao Dai (螣蛇矯逮)**: Gui + Ding. Emotional entanglements, fire hazards, paranoia.
- **Tombs (Mu 墓)**: Xing Mu (Star Tomb), Ri Mu (Day Tomb), Yue Mu (Month Tomb). Traps and suppresses Qi.

### 4.4 Timeliness Modifiers

- **Ri Wang / Yue Wang / Xing Wang**: Day, Month, or Star at peak strength; amplifies qualities.
- **Ri Shi / Yue Shi / Xing Shi**: Envoy/Messenger activation of functional affairs.
- **Ri Zhi / Xing Zhi**: Structural suppression of palace elements.

## 7. Methodological Exceptions & Flying Star Discrepancies

- **High-Rise Balcony Rule vs. Whole Building Facing** (p. a10):
  - Classical Flying Stars generally uses the entire building block's facade for all units.
  - Qi Men Feng Shui explicitly requires using the individual apartment unit's **main Balcony facing direction**. Units in the same building facing different sides have distinct Qi Men House Charts.
- **Angular Precision Discrepancy** (p. a12, a17):
  - House Facing requires only the 8 broad 45-degree directions (Trigrams).
  - Main Door Location strictly requires exact 15-degree 24-Mountain precision.
- **Door Location vs. Door Facing Pitfall** (p. a19):
  - Practitioners must never align the chart to where the front door faces outward. The chart depends strictly on the physical sector where the door aperture sits relative to the house center.
- **Period Move-in Date Rule** (p. a8):
  - The house period is determined strictly by the year the occupants moved in, regardless of construction date.

## 8. Gotchas & Best Practices

- **Default Method Enforcement**: Always calculate dynamic forecasting hour charts using **Zhi Run Fa (置閏法)** by default. Never switch to Chai Bu Fa (拆補法) or Mao Shan Fa (茅山法) unless explicitly requested by the user.
- Never count elements or doors. Qi Men is a qualitative spatial matrix, not an elemental balance tally.
- Do not mix the Hour Chart (Forecasting) with the Yang Mansion House Chart (Audit). Use the Hour Chart for remote diagnosis of an inquiry, and the House Chart for physical layout audits.
- Always check for Void (Kong Wang) and Fu Yin/Fan Yin before pronouncing a sector auspicious or inauspicious.
- In multi-generational homes, evaluate the Master Bedroom for the primary couple, the Old Folk's Room for seniors, and the Children's Bedroom for youth rather than applying a single blanket diagnosis.
- Reject commercial object remedies. Optimize spatial allocations (Man Luck) and align human activities to supportive palaces.
