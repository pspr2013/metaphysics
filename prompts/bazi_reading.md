# BaZi Reading and Prediction (PSPR)

A professional system for analyzing Four Pillars of Destiny (BaZi) charts, evaluating element quality and character potential, and generating strategic, action-oriented guidance based on the complete five-book Destiny Code and Power of X curriculum.

## Summary

This skill guides the end-to-end decoding of a BaZi chart. It integrates chart calculation, Day Master evaluation, the 10 Gods quality qualification hierarchy (Superior, Good, Average, Poor), Earthly Branch interactions, dynamic luck cycles, and practical life applications across career, wealth, relationships, and health.

## When to Use

Use this skill when the user:

- Asks to calculate, plot, read, or interpret a BaZi chart.
- Requests Day Master identification and character profiling.
- Asks to assess the strength and quality of the Five Elements or Ten Gods.
- Inquires about Missing Elements, Absent Stars, or Graveyard/Storage dynamics.
- Asks about Earthly Branch interactions (Clashes, Combinations, Punishments, Harms, Destructions).
- Analyzes 10-Year Luck Pillars (Da Yun) or Annual Pillars (Liu Nian).
- Seeks practical, non-superstitious strategic advice for career, wealth, romance, or health.

## 1. Calculation Workflow

Follow this sequence to formulate a chart with precision:

1. Confirm birth data: Gregorian birth date (year, month, day), birth hour (converted to local solar time), and gender.

2. Solar Calendar (Xia Li) conversion:

   - The solar year begins strictly on Li Chun (Coming of Spring, around February 4), never on Lunar New Year.
   - Solar month boundaries align with the 24 Solar Terms (Jie Qi), typically falling between the 4th and 8th of each Western month.
   - Early Rat hour (00:00-01:00) belongs to the current day; Late Rat hour (23:00-24:00) transitions to the next day's pillar.

3. Construct the Four Pillars (right to left: Year, Month, Day, Hour):

   - Year Pillar: Ancestors, grandparents, early childhood (ages 0-17), external market/social reputation.
   - Month Pillar: Parents, superiors, career environment, youth/early adulthood (ages 18-35). Houses the Month Branch (Yue Ling), the Season Master.
   - Day Pillar: Day Master (Heavenly Stem) represents the Self. Day Branch is the Spouse Palace, representing inner thoughts, home, and middle age (ages 36-60).
   - Hour Pillar: Children, subordinates, investments, proprietary ideas, ambitions, and later life (ages 61+).

4. Map Hidden Stems (Cang Gan):

   - Four Cardinals (pure Qi): Zi (Gui), Mao (Yi), You (Xin), Wu (Ding main Qi, Ji sub-Qi).
   - Four Growths (nascent Qi): Yin (Jia main, Bing sub, Wu sub), Si (Bing main, Geng sub, Wu sub), Shen (Geng main, Ren sub, Wu sub), Hai (Ren main, Jia sub).
   - Four Graveyards/Storages (mixed Earth Qi): Chen (Wu main, Yi sub, Gui sub), Xu (Wu main, Xin sub, Ding sub), Chou (Ji main, Gui sub, Xin sub), Wei (Ji main, Ding sub, Yi sub).
   - Polarity rule: Never confuse physical branch polarity with hidden stem Qi polarity. Wu is a Yang branch with Yin Fire (Ding) main Qi; Hai is a Yin branch with Yang Water (Ren) main Qi.

5. Dynamic cycles:

   - Determine Luck Pillar direction: Yang Male / Yin Female runs forward from Month Pillar. Yin Male / Yang Female runs in reverse.
   - Luck Pillars function as unified 10-year environmental blocks, not rigid 5-year splits.

## 2. Analysis Hierarchy

Execute analysis systematically across five tiered levels:

### Level 1: Day Master Strength and Climate Regulation

- Evaluate Day Master against Month Branch (Yue Ling):

  - Spring (Yin, Mao, Chen): Wood Prosperous (Wang); Fire Strong (Xiang); Water Weak (Xiu); Metal Trapped (Qiu); Earth Dead (Si).
  - Summer (Si, Wu, Wei): Fire Prosperous; Earth Strong/Prosperous; Wood Weak; Water Trapped; Metal Dead.
  - Autumn (Shen, You, Xu): Metal Prosperous; Water Strong; Earth Weak; Fire Trapped; Wood Dead.
  - Winter (Hai, Zi, Chou): Water Prosperous; Wood Strong; Metal Weak; Earth Trapped; Fire Dead.

- Assess secondary support (rooting and resource).

- Classify Day Master as Strong or Weak to establish baseline favorable/unfavorable categories.

- Climate regulation (Tiao Hou): Winter charts require Fire for warmth; scorching Summer charts require Water for cooling.

### Level 2: Ten Gods Quality Qualification

Assess the quality tier of each 10 God using the four-tier hierarchy:

1. Superior Quality:

   - Supported by Season of Birth as the Prosperous (Wang) element.
   - Rooted in the same polarity within the Main Qi of the same pillar.
   - Rooted in the Sub-Qi of its own Growth Branch (Yin, Si, Shen, Hai) within the same pillar.
   - Supported by a full Three Harmony Frame or full Directional Frame.

2. Good Quality:

   - Supported by Season of Birth as the secondary Strong (Xiang) element.
   - Rooted in the same polarity in Main Qi of an adjacent pillar.
   - Rooted in its own Growth Branch in an adjacent pillar.
   - Rooted in opposite polarity in Main Qi of the same pillar.
   - Supported by a partial Combination Frame plus secondary rooting or proximity.

3. Average Quality:

   - Rooted in opposite polarity within the Sub-Qi of the same pillar.
   - Rooted in opposite polarity within Sub-Qi of a Graveyard Branch in an adjacent pillar.
   - Solely reliant on Resource support without seasonal strength or rooting.

4. Poor Quality:

   - Appears only on the Heavenly Stem without branch roots, unsupported by season or resource.
   - Trapped solely in the Sub-Qi of its own Graveyard Branch without seasonal support or stem protrusion.
   - Severely countered, blunted, or severed by neighboring elements.

### Level 3: Usability, Interdependence, and Preferences

Evaluate if the Day Master can effectively wield the Star:

- Interdependence rules:

  - Geng Metal requires Ding Fire to be forged into a useful tool.
  - Ding Fire requires Jia Wood to sustain burning.
  - Xin Metal requires Ren Water to wash and reveal brilliance (Xin dislikes excessive Earth smothering).
  - Bing Fire and Ren Water enjoy mutual reflection (sunlight reflecting across vast water).
  - Jia Wood requires Geng Metal to chop it into usable timber once strong.
  - Yi Wood requires Jia Wood to climb toward the sun.

- Usability rule: An element of Good quality that is unfavorable or inaccessible to the Day Master acts as formidable competition or an unmanageable demand.

### Level 4: Earthly Branch Interactions

Analyze interactions strictly between proximate/adjacent branches:

- Six Combinations (Liu He): Union, close alliances, collaborative outcomes.
- Three Harmony Frames (San He): Shen-Zi-Chen (Water), Hai-Mao-Wei (Wood), Yin-Wu-Xu (Fire), Si-You-Chou (Metal). Strong collective networks.
- Directional Frames (San Hui): Yin-Mao-Chen (Spring Wood), Si-Wu-Wei (Summer Fire), Shen-You-Xu (Autumn Metal), Hai-Zi-Chou (Winter Water). Overwhelming seasonal dominance.
- Six Clashes (Liu Chong): Zi-Wu, Chou-Wei, Yin-Shen, Mao-You, Chen-Xu, Si-Hai. Turbulence, sudden change, displacement.
- Four Punishments (Xing): Ungrateful (Yin-Si-Shen), Bullying (Chou-Xu-Wei), Uncivilized (Zi-Mao), Self-Punishment (Chen-Chen, Wu-Wu, You-You, Hai-Hai).
- Six Harms (Liu Hai): Zi-Wei, Chou-Wu, Yin-Si, Mao-Chen, Shen-Hai, You-Xu. Hidden emotional hurt, subtle betrayal.
- Six Destructions (Liu Po): Zi-You, Wu-Mao, Chen-Chou, Si-Shen, Yin-Hai, Xu-Wei. Breaking down obsolete structures.

### Level 5: Strategic Life Applications

Translate structural findings into practical counsel:

- Career: Map modern talent demands (Output for innovation, Wealth for execution, Influence for management).
- Wealth: Distinguish salary/asset stability (Direct Wealth) from entrepreneurial deals (Indirect Wealth).
- Relationships: Evaluate Spouse Palace stability and Spouse Star quality.
- Health: Trace elemental imbalances and check Direct Resource as vitality barometer.

## 4. Conflict-Handling Rules

When chart elements present contradictory indications, resolve them using these hierarchical rules:

1. Quality overrides Quantity: Never count elements. Three floating, unrooted Wealth stars are weaker and of poorer quality than one rooted, in-season Wealth star.

2. Season overrides Minor Interactions: An element supported by season has structural resilience. A clash against an in-season, rooted element causes turbulence but does not destroy the element. A clash against an unrooted, out-of-season element shatters it.

3. Favourability qualifies Quality:

   - Good Quality + Favourable Star: Maximum positive manifestation and rapid compounding of success.
   - Good Quality + Unfavourable Star: Formidable obstacle or fierce competitor.
   - Poor Quality + Unfavourable Star: Minor nuisance; harmless or even advantageous (e.g., weak Rob Wealth cannot steal wealth).
   - Poor Quality + Favourable Star: Frustration; the person aspires to use the skill but lacks the capacity or produces half-baked results.

4. Resource Production Efficiency:

   - Efficient: Wood feeding Fire; Metal producing Water.
   - Inefficient/Obstructive: Dry Earth (Xu, Wei) smothers Metal instead of producing it. Only moist Earth (Chou, Chen) nurtures Metal.
   - Reverse Growth: Bing Fire does not draw strength from Wood; the Sun nourishes trees rather than trees strengthening the Sun.

5. Graveyard vs. Storage Disambiguation:

   - Graveyard (Mu): Chen (Wood), Wei (Fire), Xu (Metal), Chou (Water). Qi is waning and trapped.
   - Storage (Ku): Chen (Water), Wei (Wood), Xu (Fire), Chou (Metal). Qi is preserved in reserve.
   - Out of Graveyard Rule: An element in its Graveyard branch is poor quality only if it appears nowhere else. If it protrudes onto the Heavenly Stems, it is released and active.

6. Time of Birth Qualifying Fire:

   - Ding Fire thrives in darkness (night/dawn hours such as Tiger, Rooster, Rat), representing an illuminating candle.
   - Bing Fire requires daytime (Dragon, Snake, Horse hours) to shine as the Sun; at Rooster hour (dusk) or Rat hour (midnight), Bing is downgraded regardless of seasonal support.

## 5. Quality-Control Rules

Enforce these strict standards during every reading:

1. Rejection of Superstition: Strictly reject amulets, crystals, colors, trinkets, or name alterations. BaZi reflects astronomical Qi. The only authentic remedy is Man Luck (conscious choice, behavioral shifts, discipline, and strategic timing).

2. The Missing Means Missing Rule: If an element is absent from both Stems and Branches, its quality is zero. Do not fabricate remedies. Analyze the knock-on systemic breaks in the Five Element cycle and instruct the user to focus on their present, high-quality stars.

3. Health Reading Guardrails:

   - Direct Resource is the master vitality indicator.
   - Weak/Poor Direct Resource indicates low stamina, lethargy, and vulnerability to clashes.
   - Overly Prosperous Direct Resource indicates Qi Stagnation and hypersensitive immunity.
   - Missing Resource indicates absent early-warning symptoms; recommend regular medical screenings.
   - Never provide definitive medical diagnoses; frame observations around constitutional tendencies.

4. Clear Demarcation of Life Spheres: Always distinguish internal character (Day Master/Companion) from operational capabilities (Output), commercial opportunities (Wealth), structural authority (Influence), and knowledge assets (Resource).
