# Qi Men and Personal Date Selection

A professional, non-superstitious timing and tactical activation system that synthesizes Personal Date Selection (Ze Ri / 择日) with Qi Men Dun Jia Date Selection (奇门择日) to align human endeavors with macro-astronomical Qi and directional forces.

## Summary

This skill executes precision date selection by combining the subject's personal BaZi chart with traditional calendar algorithms (12 Day Officers, 28 Constellations, Dong Gong, universal and personal taboos) and hourly Qi Men Dun Jia plates (8 Doors, 9 Stars, 8 Deities, 3 Nobles). It ensures that every chosen date is safe from afflictions, resonant with personal favorable elements, and empowered by spatial Qi activation.

## When to Use

Use this skill when the user:

- Asks to select an auspicious date and time for significant milestones: business launches, signing contracts, pitch meetings, negotiations, weddings, property purchases, moving into a home (Ru Zhai), renovations, or medical procedures.
- Inquires about Personal Date Selection (harmonizing calendar dates with personal BaZi Day Master and Day/Year branches).
- Asks for Qi Men Dun Jia Date Selection (奇门择日) to identify the optimal double-hour, door, star, and compass direction for strategic action.
- Seeks to screen out taboos such as Sui Po, Yue Po, Ri Po, San Sha, Si Li, Si Jue, Men Po, or Kong Wang.
- Asks how to execute directional activations (Back to Direction, Moving Towards Direction, Space Activation).

## 1. Calculation Workflow

Execute timing selection through this five-stage chronological pipeline:

### 1.1 Personal BaZi Baseline Formulation
1. Confirm client's birth data: Gregorian year, month, day, exact birth hour, and gender.
2. Formulate the Four Pillars using Local Solar Time and the Solar Calendar (*Xia Li*, starting strictly at *Li Chun*).
3. Identify critical personal parameters:
   - **Day Master** (Self Element) and baseline Favourable/Useful Elements (*Xi Shen / Yong Shen*).
   - **Day Branch** (Spouse Palace / Personal Core) and **Year Branch** (Outer Foundation / Market).
   - **Personal Shen Sha**: Heavenly Nobleman (*Tian Yi Gui Ren*), Prosperity Star (*Lu Shen*), and Traveling Horse (*Yi Ma*).

### 1.2 Event Profiling & Activity Type
Classify the activity into its primary functional category:
- **Commercial & Expansion**: Product launch, company incorporation, grand opening, marketing campaigns, major investments.
- **Transactional & Legal**: Contract signing, partnership execution, VIP negotiations, legal settlements.
- **Relational & Life**: Wedding/marriage registration, housewarming/move-in (*Ru Zhai*), ground-breaking (*Dong Tu*).
- **Cleansing & Resolving**: Medical surgery, debt collection, terminating unprofitable partnerships, demolition.

### 1.3 Universal Taboo Elimination (Macro Filter)
Screen target dates to immediately disqualify calendar-wide afflictions:
1. **Sui Po (岁破 - Year Breaker)**: Days clashing with the current Year Branch.
2. **Yue Po (月破 - Month Breaker)**: Days clashing with the current Month Branch.
3. **San Sha (三煞 - Three Killings)**: Days carrying Robbery Sha, Calamity Sha, or Annual Sha relative to the current year/season.
4. **Si Li (四离 - Four Separation Days)**: Days immediately preceding the Vernal Equinox, Autumnal Equinox, Summer Solstice, and Winter Solstice.
5. **Si Jue (四绝 - Four Exhaustion Days)**: Days immediately preceding Li Chun, Li Xia, Li Qiu, and Li Dong.
6. **Yang Gong Shi San Ji (杨公十三忌 - Master Yang's 13 Disastrous Days)**.

### 1.4 Personal BaZi Harmonization (Micro Filter)
Filter remaining candidate days against the user's specific natal blueprint:
1. **Personal Sui Po Elimination**: Disqualify any day clashing with the user's natal Year Branch.
2. **Personal Ri Po (Day Breaker) Elimination**: Strictly disqualify any day clashing with the user's natal Day Branch (Spouse Palace / Self).
3. **Element Support**: Prioritize days carrying Heavenly Stems or Earthly Branches that represent the user's Favourable Elements (*Yong Shen / Xi Shen*).
4. **12 Day Officer Matching (*Jian Chu 十二建除*)**:
   - *Cheng (Success 成) & Kai (Open 开)*: Optimal for launches, weddings, contract execution, openings.
   - *Ding (Settle 定) & Man (Full 满)*: Optimal for long-term agreements, asset gathering, appointing key personnel.
   - *Chu (Remove 除)*: Optimal for surgeries, medical treatments, ending contracts, demolition.
   - *Jian (Establish 建)*: Executive inaugurations, proposals, strategic initiatives.
   - *Po (Destruction 破), Wei (Danger 危), Bi (Close 闭)*: Strictly avoid for launches or celebrations.
5. **28 Lunar Mansions (*Er Shi Ba Xiu 二十八宿*) & Dong Gong System**: Confirm the constellation and Yellow Path (*Huang Dao*) vs. Black Path (*Hei Dao*) spirits favor the intended action.

### 1.5 Qi Men Dun Jia Hourly Selection & Spatial Activation
For vetted days, cast and evaluate Qi Men Dun Jia hour charts (*Shi Jia Qi Men*):
1. **Determine Dun Structure & Active Ju (Default: Zhi Run Fa 置閏法)**:
   - Calculate all Qi Men Dun Jia charts using the **Zhi Run Fa (置閏法 - Intercalation Method)** as the mandatory default system (unless the user explicitly requests Chai Bu Fa or Mao Shan Fa).
   - **Yuan Cycle Allocation**:
     - **Upper Yuan (Shang Yuan 上元)**: Jia/Ji days with Zi, Wu, Mao, You (子午卯酉).
     - **Middle Yuan (Zhong Yuan 中元)**: Jia/Ji days with Yin, Shen, Si, Hai (寅申巳亥).
     - **Lower Yuan (Xia Yuan 下元)**: Jia/Ji days with Chen, Xu, Chou, Wei (辰戌丑未).
     - Each Yuan strictly spans 5 days (60 double-hours / 1 Fu 符).
   - **Intercalation Mechanics (Zhi Run 置閏)**:
     - Compare the arrival of the Jia/Ji leader day against the entry of the 24 Solar Terms (*Jie Qi*).
     - Identify **Zheng Shou (正授)** (exact match), **Chao Shen (超神)** (stem cycle leads solar node), or **Jie Qi (接氣)** (solar node leads stem cycle).
     - When Chao Shen exceeds 9 days (reaching 10–14 days) before the Summer Solstice (*Xia Zhi*) or Winter Solstice (*Dong Zhi*), execute **Zhi Run**: repeat the entire 15-day 3-Yuan cycle of the term preceding the Solstice (*Mang Zhong* before Xia Zhi, *Da Xue* before Dong Zhi) to realign macro solar terms with the 60 Jia Zi cycle before changing Dun direction.
   - **Dun Polarity & Ju Sequence**:
     - **Yang Dun (陽遁)**: Winter Solstice (*Dong Zhi*) to Summer Solstice (*Xia Zhi*) — Ju numbers proceed forward (1 to 9).
     - **Yin Dun (陰遁)**: Summer Solstice (*Xia Zhi*) to Winter Solstice (*Dong Zhi*) — Ju numbers proceed in reverse (9 to 1).
2. Map the Nine Palaces across the 4 plates: Deities (*Ba Shen*), Stars (*Jiu Xing*), Doors (*Ba Men*), and Stems (*Tian Pan / Di Pan*).
3. Select the Palace containing the primary auspicious door for the objective:
   - **Life Door (生门 - Earth 8)**: Profit generation, capital deployment, real estate transactions, commercial growth.
   - **Open Door (开门 - Metal 6)**: Business launches, career advancements, company incorporation, public debuts.
   - **Rest Door (休门 - Water 1)**: Negotiations, diplomatic talks, relationship harmony, noble assistance, healing.
   - **Scenery Door (景门 - Fire 9)**: PR campaigns, brand launches, media appearances, content releases.
4. Verify the presence of the Three Nobles (*San Qi*):
   - *Yi Qi (Sun Noble)*: Smooth settlements, reconciliation, gradual expansion.
   - *Bing Qi (Moon Noble)*: Authority, dramatic breakthrough, rapid prestige.
   - *Ding Qi (Star Noble)*: Legal contracts, intellectual focus, high-precision outcomes.
5. Filter Qi Men afflictions: Target palace must be free of **Men Po (Door Compulsion)**, **Gong Po (Palace Compulsion)**, **Kong Wang (Void)**, **Ru Mu (Graveyard/Tomb)**, and **Liu Yi Ji Xing (Six Crises)**.

## 2. Analysis Hierarchy

Structure every timing recommendation through this 5-tier evaluation hierarchy:

### Tier 1: Personal BaZi Resonance & Goal Alignment
- Define the client's natal vulnerabilities and identify the exact Yong Shen needed to power the event.
- Map the target outcome to its elemental and thematic archetype.

### Tier 2: Macro Temporal Safety
- Verify absolute clearance from universal annual and monthly afflictions (Sui Po, Yue Po, San Sha, Si Li, Si Jue).

### Tier 3: Individual Astrological Harmony
- Enforce the non-negotiable personal clash filter (Zero Personal Ri Po, Zero Personal Sui Po).
- Confirm alignment with 12 Day Officers, 28 Mansions, and personal Shen Sha (Tian Yi Gui Ren, Lu Shen).

### Tier 4: Micro Qi Men Dun Jia Hourly Empowerment
- Qualify the hour chart: Auspicious Door + Three Nobles + Auspicious Formation (*Qing Long Tao Zhou*, *Fei Niao Die Xue*, *San Dun*).
- Confirm absence of internal palace structural defects (Men Po, Kong Wang).

### Tier 5: Physical Execution & Directional Alignment
- Translate the chosen palace into real-world action:
  - **Back to Direction**: Sit facing away from the designated compass direction (back towards the auspicious palace) during pitches, phone calls, negotiations, or decision-making.
  - **Move Towards Direction**: Travel in the direction of the auspicious palace for critical in-person meetings.
  - **Space Activation**: Execute physical activities or placement in the corresponding property sector.

## 4. Conflict-Handling Rules

When chart indications present contradictory signals, resolve them using these hierarchical rules:

1. **Personal BaZi Clash Overrides Universal Auspiciousness**:
   - Even if a date possesses *Cheng (Success)* officer, supreme Huang Dao stars, and top Qi Men formations, if it clashes with the client's Day Branch (*Personal Ri Po*) or Year Branch (*Personal Sui Po*), it is **strictly unusable**.
2. **Door Compulsion (Men Po) Veto**:
   - An auspicious door entering a palace that it counters (*Men Po*) or that counters it (*Gong Po*) loses its beneficial Qi and generates friction. Never select a Men Po palace for high-stakes initiatives.
3. **Void Palace (Kong Wang) Discount**:
   - When an auspicious palace falls into Kong Wang, discount its tangible effectiveness by 70–80%. Deals struck or launches executed under Kong Wang tend to encounter hollow promises, delays, or phantom results.
4. **Macro Safety Overrides Micro Hour Qi**:
   - Never use an auspicious Qi Men hour on a day compromised by Sui Po or Yue Po. The day's foundation carries macro stability; an hour cannot redeem a fundamentally afflicted day.
5. **Yong Shen Harmony Over General Element Strength**:
   - A day featuring the client's personal Favourable Element (*Yong Shen*) provides far more sustainable momentum than a universally celebrated day that introduces personal unfavourable elements.
6. **Default Charting Method (Zhi Run Fa Priority)**:
   - When calculating Ju and Dun structures for date selection, strictly apply **Zhi Run Fa (置閏法)** by default. Disregard Chai Bu Fa (拆補法) or Mao Shan Fa (茅山法) unless the user explicitly requests one of those alternative systems.
