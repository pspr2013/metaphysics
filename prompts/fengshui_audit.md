# Feng Shui Homebuyer Audit

A structured system for screening, auditing, and optimizing residential homes, land plots, and floor plans based on Phann Sophearith's *Feng Shui for Homebuyers — Exterior* (pp. 1-299) and *Feng Shui for Homebuyers — Interior* (pp. 1-299).

## When to Use

- Screening residential properties before buying, renting, or signing contracts.
- Auditing external landforms, surrounding roads, water bodies, and elevations.
- Evaluating building architecture, roof profiles, missing sectors, and property gates.
- Assessing internal layouts: Main Door, Kitchen/Stove, Bedroom/Bed, and Staircases.
- Calculating Eight Mansions (Ba Zhai) House Gua and occupant Life Gua interactions.
- Resolving spatial conflicts between external landform influences and mathematical formulas.
- Demystifying commercial or superstitious claims (plants, colors, mirrors, rulers).

## Analysis Hierarchy

Always execute property audits from the outside in. Never evaluate interior rooms until the macro and micro environments pass inspection.

1. **Macro Environment (1-2 km radius):** Topography, mountain dragons, major water bodies, and primary transit corridors [Exterior, pp. 49, 51-57].
2. **Micro Neighborhood (500 m radius on foot):** Immediate roads, intersections, neighboring structures, utility installations, and localized Sha Qi [Exterior, pp. 173-174; Interior, pp. 62, 70].
3. **Land Plot (The Macro Qi Container):** Geometry, contour elevations, drainage, and subsurface soil quality [Exterior, pp. 58-91].
4. **Building Architecture (The Physical Container):** Facade balance, roof profiles, footprint geometry, perimeter fences, and gates [Exterior, pp. 175-213].
5. **The Three Key Internal Factors (San Yao):** Main Door, Kitchen/Stove, and Master Bedroom/Bed [Interior, pp. 58, 63-238].
6. **Secondary Internal Circulations:** Central Palace (Heavenly Heart), staircases, internal water/aquariums, and missing sectors [Interior, pp. 46-57, 241-272].
7. **Occupant Alignment:** Matching individual Life Gua directions to optimized House Gua room sectors [Interior, pp. 14-19, 42-45].

## Calculation Workflows

### 1. Facing Direction Protocol
- Identify the architectural facade (the primary frontage the building was designed to face), not necessarily the main entrance door [Exterior, pp. 29-30; Interior, pp. 21-23].
- Stand at the Central Palace of the facade (Zhong Gong), face outward toward the street, and take the compass reading [Exterior, p. 31; Interior, p. 24].
- If the Main Door is on the side or recessed, the building facade still determines the house orientation [Exterior, p. 30; Interior, p. 23].

### 2. Spatial Demarcation System
- **Exterior Evaluation (8-Pie Method):** Radiate eight 45-degree sectors from the center point across the site map. External space has no physical walls; hence, there is no enclosed central palace [Exterior, pp. 32, 35-37].
- **Interior Evaluation (9-Palaces Grid):** Superimpose a 3x3 grid over the interior blueprint. Interior Qi pools and circulates by room walls and enclosures [Exterior, pp. 32-33; Interior, pp. 25-26].

### 3. Eight Mansions (Ba Zhai) Derivation
Determine the House Gua based on the Sitting sector (opposite the Facing):
- Sitting North (Facing South) = Kan House (#1 Water, East Group) [Interior, p. 28].
- Sitting East (Facing West) = Zhen House (#3 Wood, East Group) [Interior, p. 29].
- Sitting Southeast (Facing Northwest) = Xun House (#4 Wood, East Group) [Interior, p. 30].
- Sitting South (Facing North) = Li House (#9 Fire, East Group) [Interior, p. 31].
- Sitting Southwest (Facing Northeast) = Kun House (#2 Earth, West Group) [Interior, p. 32].
- Sitting Northwest (Facing Southeast) = Qian House (#6 Metal, West Group) [Interior, p. 33].
- Sitting West (Facing East) = Dui House (#7 Metal, West Group) [Interior, p. 34].
- Sitting Northeast (Facing Southwest) = Gen House (#8 Earth, West Group) [Interior, p. 35].

Calculate occupant Life Gua from the solar birth year (counting from February 4):
- Male Life Gua formula: (100 - last 2 digits of birth year) % 9. (For births 2000+, formula adjusts to (99 - last 2 digits) % 9). 5 becomes 2 (Kun) [Interior, pp. 15-16].
- Female Life Gua formula: (last 2 digits of birth year - 4) % 9. (For births 2000+, formula adjusts to (last 2 digits + 6) % 9). 5 becomes 8 (Gen) [Interior, pp. 15-16].

### 4. Kitchen "Sit Inauspicious, Face Auspicious" (Zuo Xiong Xiang Ji)
- Position the kitchen/stove inside an inauspicious sector of the House Gua (Huo Hai, Wu Gui, Liu Sha, or Jue Ming) [Interior, pp. 164-171].
- The cooking Fire suppresses and consumes the negative Qi of that sector [Interior, p. 164].
- Orient the stove knobs/controls (the Firemouth) to face one of the primary cook's favorable Life Gua directions (Sheng Qi, Tian Yi, Yan Nian, or Fu Wei) [Interior, pp. 164-171].

### 5. Missing Sector Mathematical Threshold (1/3 Rule)
- Overlay the 3x3 9-Palaces grid onto the house blueprint [Interior, pp. 47-48].
- An indentation is classified as a missing sector only if the missing portion exceeds one-third (1/3) of that sector's width or depth [Exterior, pp. 199-200; Interior, pp. 47-48].
- Indentations smaller than 1/3 are minor architectural recesses and do not damage the sector's Trigram [Interior, p. 47].
- Unenclosed carports are excluded from the grid. Enclosed garages are excluded unless converted into primary heated living space [Interior, pp. 48-49].

## Source-Reference & Diagnostic System

### External Landforms & Surroundings
- **Period 6-9 Mountain Rule (1964-2043):** Auspicious for mountains in Northwest, West, Northeast, and South. Inauspicious for mountains in East, Southwest, Southeast, and North [Exterior, p. 94].
- **Water Forms:** Sentimental curving water gathers wealth Qi; straight, fast, rushing water (Merciless Water) drains wealth [Exterior, pp. 144-147]. Cascading terraces (Jin Tian Shui) bring multi-generational wealth [Exterior, pp. 148-149].
- **Subsurface Soil Testing:** Dig 1-2 feet below topsoil. Superior soils: Greedy Wolf (greenish / status), Huge Door (yellowish / wealth), Military Arts (whitish / power) [Exterior, pp. 88-90].
- **Piercing Heart Sha (Chuan Xin Sha):** Project parallel lines from the Main Door jambs outward. A post, tree, or pillar is hazardous only if it stands within this projected path [Interior, pp. 70-72, 80].
- **High-Voltage Electrical Pylons:** Dangerous within 100 feet or when facing the door. Activates when the annual Grand Duke (Tai Sui) reaches the pylon's 24-Mountain compass sector [Exterior, pp. 226-227].
- **Crack in the Sky Sha (Tian Zhan Sha):** Narrow wind gap between two tall structures funnels cutting Qi. Afflicts the corresponding Trigram (Chen/SE = legs; Gen/NE = spine/back) [Exterior, pp. 228-229; Interior, p. 78].
- **Curved Knife Roads (Fan Gong Sha):** Outer curved edge cuts the house with Blade Sha; inner curve embraces and protects [Exterior, pp. 152-153].
- **Peeping Tom Sha (Tan Tou Feng):** Peak or structure peeking over a closer ridge/roofline. Triggers theft, fraud, and embezzlement when paired with 3-7 or 7-3 Flying Star combinations [Exterior, pp. 231-233].
- **White Tiger Sha:** Aggressive sharp structures on the right-hand side (looking out) clash with the White Tiger's Metal element, destabilizing female health and domestic peace [Exterior, pp. 234-235].

### Interior Architecture & Rooms
- **Roof Geometry:** Inward-sloping roofs pierce the Heavenly Heart (Central Palace) like a wedge. Flat (Earth), protruding (Wood), or domed (Metal) roofs are stable [Exterior, pp. 184-192].
- **L-Shaped Buildings:** The central Taiji falls outside the building envelope. Remedy: build an interior partition wall to divide the layout into two independent rectangular blocks [Exterior, pp. 197-198].
- **Perimeter Gates (Fen Jia):** Only one primary main gate is permitted. Two equal-sized gates split the Qi mouth, triggering family division or property disputes. Secondary gates must be visibly smaller [Exterior, pp. 208-209].
- **Bedroom & Bed Positioning:** Headboard must rest against a solid wall (Yin backing). Never place headboards against windows (Yang) without thick curtains [Interior, pp. 182, 186]. Never angle a bed diagonally into a corner [Interior, p. 183]. Avoid overhead beams [Interior, p. 184] and beds aligned with doorways [Interior, pp. 188-189].
- **Central Palace (Zhong Gong):** Keep quiet and stable. Never place a staircase, kitchen, toilet, or pond in the exact center of the home [Interior, pp. 243-244, 256].
- **Staircase Design:** Straight steep flights rush Qi; intermediate landings allow Qi to meander [Interior, pp. 248-249]. A bedroom door opening directly onto descending steps creates Pulling Nose Water (Qian Bi Shui), siphoning Qi away [Interior, pp. 250-252].
- **Indoor Water & Aquariums:** Pools directly over the Main Door create Wetting the Head Water (Lin Tou Shui / bankruptcy risk) [Interior, p. 257]. Safe sectors for aquariums through 2043 are Southwest, East, North, and Southeast [Interior, pp. 260-263].

## Conflict-Handling Rules

1. **Location vs. Direction:** Location (House Gua sector) always supersedes facing direction. Place occupants in favorable rooms first; personal Life Gua facing angles are secondary adjustments [Interior, pp. 42-44].
2. **Forms vs. Stars:** Favorable physical landforms suppress dormant negative stars (such as 5-2 combinations), keeping them harmless. Negative stars activate only when triggered by external or internal structural flaws [Exterior, pp. 176-178; Interior, p. 8].
3. **Fast Traffic as a Protective Buffer:** While fast traffic is generally merciless, a busy multi-lane road separating a residence from a negative neighbor (e.g., a cemetery) acts as a dynamic shield, dispersing negative Yin Qi [Exterior, p. 157; Interior, p. 216].
4. **Commercial vs. Residential Twin Stairs:** Double Dragon curved staircases split Qi and cause relationship breakups in residential homes, but are permissible in high-traffic commercial spaces (such as hotels) [Interior, p. 245].
5. **Elemental Neighbor Conflicts:** Living near a temple, gas station, or substation (Fire element) is harmful only if Fire clashes with the occupant's BaZi or the property's natal chart. Introducing a strategic water feature acts as a buffer [Exterior, pp. 218-220].

## Quality-Control & Demystification Protocols

Reject superstitious claims that lack grounding in Classical texts:
- **Plants & Cacti:** Cacti needles do not emit Sha Qi; money plants do not generate cash; mother-in-law's tongue does not cause domestic fights. Plants carry zero classical Feng Shui weight [Exterior, p. 238].
- **Decorative Art:** Sailing ships, inward-flowing waterfalls, and 9-koi fish paintings are personal aesthetic choices with no physical Qi impact [Exterior, p. 240].
- **Color Taboos:** Blue roofs do not cause drowning; red walls do not cause fires. Exterior and interior paint colors do not alter landform Qi [Exterior, p. 242].
- **Carpenter's Ruler (Lu Ban Chi):** Auspicious red measurements apply strictly to Yin House Feng Shui (graves, coffins, altars). They do not govern Yang House residential furniture [Exterior, p. 243].
- **Ba Gua Mirrors:** These are folk religious talismans. A small mirror cannot reflect or deflect topographical wind, traffic currents, or landform Sha [Exterior, p. 244; Interior, p. 88].
- **Bed Facing Mirror:** Reflective surfaces may cause psychological startle responses, but they carry no metaphysical Sha Qi or infidelity curses [Interior, p. 185].
- **Aquarium Fish Counts & Colors:** Fish merely maintain water movement (Yang); specific species, colors, or counts (e.g., eight red, one black) are folklore [Interior, p. 261].

## Gotchas & Operational Pitfalls

- **Avoid the "Cure-It" Syndrome:** Not every flaw can be fixed. If a property has severe landform Sha (e.g., pylon within 100 feet, plunging downhill slope, or V-shaped roof), the professional recommendation is avoidance, not cosmetic remedies [Exterior, pp. 7, 50; Interior, pp. 59-60, 81].
- **Don't Confuse Door with Facade:** Taking the compass reading from an angled or side door rather than the building facade skews the entire House Gua and Flying Star chart [Exterior, pp. 29-30; Interior, pp. 21-23].
- **Avoid Angled Beds:** Never angle a bed diagonally into a corner to pursue a personal direction; this exposes the headboard to corner Sha Qi [Interior, p. 183].
- **Verify Door Piercing Lines:** Never declare Piercing Heart Sha visually without checking whether the pole or tree falls within the parallel projection of the door jambs [Interior, pp. 70-71].
